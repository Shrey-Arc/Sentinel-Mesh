## Final Conclusions

Scientifically Defensible:
- Persistent mission infrastructure
- Cooperative localization
- Dock-assisted mission continuity
- Graceful degradation

Technically Feasible but Difficult:
- Autonomous docking
- Persistent fleet operation
- Cooperative localization

Weak Directions:
- Massive swarms
- Distributed AI clouds
- Exotic airframes before validation

Final recommendation:
Develop CPUIA as a persistent cooperative UAV infrastructure research platform rather than a swarm platform.
